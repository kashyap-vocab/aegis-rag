# Extended Evaluation — 100 held-out queries

A larger test set, built after all tuning was finished, to check that the design generalises beyond the 6 official queries:
- **50 answerable questions** covering every fact in the three SOPs, in four styles (direct, paraphrase, reasoning, keyword-only);
- **50 unanswerable questions** in five adversarial categories.

The gate threshold τ, the prompt and the models were **frozen** before this set was run; nothing was re-tuned on it.

- Set: `eval/extended_queries.csv` (generated by `scripts/make_extended_set.py`)
- Runner: `scripts/evaluate_extended.py`
- Raw results: `docs/results/extended_*.json`
- Hardware: laptop CPU (Ryzen 5 5500U, 6 threads), fully offline. LLMs served by Ollama with 4-bit Q4_K_M quantisation.

## Findings

1. **Retrieval generalises:** 50/50 answerable held-out questions retrieve the correct SOP first, in every configuration.
2. **The gate threshold generalises.** τ = 0.0075 was calibrated on 20 earlier queries. On the 50 unseen answerable questions it wrongly refused **none**, and it stopped 9 of 10 off-domain questions before any LLM call. Those refusals are ~10× faster than a generated answer (0.5 s vs 5.8 s).
3. **Each layer covers a different category:**
   - the gate handles off-domain questions;
   - the LLM's answerability judgement handles in-domain missing-attribute and false-premise questions;
   - the injection filter handles instruction-override attempts in under a millisecond.
4. **Model size matters most for answering, not refusing.** The 1.5B model refuses 43/50 unanswerable questions but also declines many valid direct questions (10/18 answered). The 3B model answers 100% of direct and paraphrased questions while refusing 48/50 unanswerable ones. That trade-off is why 3B is the deployed model; 1.5B remains the fallback when the primary model is unavailable.
5. **Headroom:** reasoning-style questions (64%) and keyword-only queries (57%) are the weakest categories. Two routes to improve them:
   - query rewriting for terse keyword queries;
   - allowing *derived* numbers (e.g. a computed temperature difference) through the grounding verifier when every operand is itself grounded.
